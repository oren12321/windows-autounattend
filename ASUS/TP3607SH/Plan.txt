ASUS OFFLINE DRIVER INSTALL PLAN
===========================

Download installers from official ASUS website, extract
them to the appropriate folders and run the install.bat.
The Nvidia driver requires a custom install.bat of its own
in its root folder (rename it from install.nvidia.bat to
install.bat after copying it to the extracted Nvidia installer).
